Service 01
Design & Engineering

Precision engineering from first sketch to final circuit

At MPS, we engineer every solar project with precision from the first layout sketch to the last circuit breaker. Our multi-disciplinary design team covers all aspects of solar plant engineering, ensuring seamless execution and long-term performance.

We begin with an optimised plant layout, accounting for land contours, shadow analysis, and yield maximisation — then deep-dive into electrical and structural design, balancing performance, safety, and cost.

  • Detailed voltage drop analysis and optimised cable sizing for AC and DC systems
  • Protection system design including breaker sizing and fault current calculations
  • Structure design validation: wind loads, soil characteristics, and stability checks
  • Preparation of GA drawings, SLDs, and construction-ready documentation
  • Shadow analysis, yield maximisation, and optimised plant layout
  • Standards-compliant, site-specific solutions — from 100 kW rooftop to 100 MW park

Service 02
Battery Energy Storage Systems

Complete BESS design and engineering — from sizing to commissioning

We provide complete design and engineering services for BESS, tailored to support grid stability, load shifting, and renewable integration. Our team handles every technical aspect from energy sizing and application mapping to system architecture and control strategy.

We evaluate use cases like peak shaving, backup, frequency regulation, and solar smoothing — and design for both AC and DC coupled systems, hybrid microgrids, and standalone storage projects.

  • Load and autonomy calculations for accurate system sizing
  • Battery sizing based on C-rating and daily cycle requirements
  • System voltage and inverter matching for optimal efficiency
  • Protection design including fuse and breaker coordination
  • Integration with solar PV and diesel gensets in hybrid setups
  • Fully compliant with international standards across multiple chemistries and control platforms

Service 03
Technical Documentation & Tender Support

Professionally packaged tenders — technically sound, procurement-ready

MPS provides comprehensive technical documentation services that support solar projects from feasibility to final EPC selection. Our work enables clients to float professional, technically sound tenders and make informed procurement decisions.

Whether you're an asset owner, consultant, or government body, we help define the scope clearly, align with local standards, and build a competitive, technically viable package.

  • Detailed Bill of Materials (BOM) and equipment schedules
  • Single Line Diagrams (SLDs), General Arrangement (GA), and layout drawings
  • Design basis reports, performance simulations, and preliminary yield forecasts
  • Site-specific technical feasibility reports and grid compliance studies
  • Tender documents: Scope of Work, Performance Guarantees, and Evaluation Criteria
  • EPC filtering support — technical clarifications, scoring matrix, and bid comparisons

Installation & Commissioning Support

On-ground expertise — built right, built to last

MPS provides on-ground support for installation and commissioning of solar power systems, tailored to the needs of each client and project. Whether acting as a turnkey execution partner or offering dedicated commissioning support, we deploy highly skilled engineers, supervisors, and technical specialists.

Our support model adapts to the project — from full-scope EPC execution to last-mile commissioning assistance across varied geographies.

Service 05
O&M, Reporting & Predictive Maintenance

Intelligent O&M — keeping your plant at peak every hour, every day

MPS offers intelligent Operations & Maintenance solutions that go beyond routine inspections and cleaning. Our approach integrates real-time monitoring, data-driven performance analysis, and predictive maintenance — ensuring issues are identified and resolved before they impact generation.

We maintain solar assets across utility, commercial, and industrial segments, with remote teams managing plants across multiple time zones.

  • Preventive maintenance plans based on OEM standards and site conditions
  • Daily, monthly, and annual performance reporting with KPI tracking
  • Predictive diagnostics: inverter-level data, IV curve analysis, and weather correlation
  • Root cause analysis for underperformance and technical losses
  • Cleaning schedules, vegetation control, and physical inspections
  • In-house SCADA analysis, string-level tracking, and event response
  • Warranty claim coordination, spares inventory management, and ticketing

Service 06
Balance of System (BOS) Supply

Quality-assured BOS supply — every component, on spec, on time

In addition to engineering and project execution, MPS offers reliable and timely Balance of System supply to support seamless solar plant construction. We partner with certified manufacturers and global suppliers to deliver high-quality components meeting both performance and compliance standards across geographies.

Our BOS supply is fully integrated with our design team, ensuring engineering compatibility and cost control at every step.

  • DC Cables, AC Cables, Earthing & Communication Cables
  • DCDBs, ACDBs, AJBs, and LT Panels custom-built to project specs
  • MC4 connectors, cable trays, and junction boxes
  • Module mounting structures — HDG, pre-galvanised, or aluminium
  • Inverter stations, combiner boxes, and surge protection devices
  • Transformers, isolators, RMUs, and other grid-interface components
  • SCADA enclosures, weather stations, and instrumentation
