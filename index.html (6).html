What we do

Comprehensive solar engineering services

From initial concept to long-term operation, our team delivers engineering excellence at every stage of the solar lifecycle.

Service 01
Design & Engineering

Smart, scalable engineering for rooftop, ground-mount, floating, and carport systems — performance from day one, built to last.

Service 02
Battery Energy Storage (BESS)

Tailored AC/DC coupled storage solutions for energy shifting, peak shaving, and microgrid stability — optimised to your grid and ROI.

Service 03
Technical Docs & Tender Support

From BOQ to bid — complete engineering documentation, tender packages, and technical vetting for EPC selection and procurement.

Service 04
Installation & Commissioning

End-to-end support during construction, including IFC drawings, engineering coordination, and commissioning compliance checks.

Service 05
O&M & Predictive Maintenance

Daily to yearly performance tracking, root cause diagnostics, and maintenance planning driven by real-time and big-data insights.

Service 06
Balance of System (BOS) Supply

Cables, panels, structures, protections, and SCADA gear — every critical component beyond modules and inverters, quality-assured.
